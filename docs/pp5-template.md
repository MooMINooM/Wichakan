# PP5 form reference

Reference: user-supplied `report5_V29.xls(2).xlsx`, print sheets `1.พิมพ์หน้าปก`, `2.พิมพ์เวลาเรียน`, `3.พิมพ์คะแนน`, `5.พิมพ์ ตชว`.

The printed form retains the source's emblem/title, inline course metadata, grade/count/percentage table, side-by-side assessment summaries, approval/signature boxes, four-level attendance headers and ruled 45-row rosters. Scores use portrait A4 with five main score columns (coursework, midterm, final, total and grade), eight traits and five reading criteria. Indicators retain the metadata and numbered writing area. Sarabun is embedded in the PDF.

User-directed adaptations take precedence over the original template: attendance has 25 Monday–Friday date columns in each five-week block, with multiple actual timetable periods in the same day cell. Nonteaching days are shaded. Dates beyond the first 20 weeks and rosters beyond 45 students continue rather than being cropped. Page numbers apply to the full book.

Scores/caps come directly from existing system records, in the same order as the teacher's score-entry form: coursework, midterm, final, total and grade. The user explicitly requested removing indicator-level score columns, pre/post-midterm breakdowns, retake placeholders and derived in-term totals. Indicators are report content on the separate indicator page, not score-entry requirements or grading inputs. Annual primary grades remain pending until both terms are complete.

The original workbook's fixed staff names are not copied into live reports: current teacher/homeroom names come from system data, while unconfigured approval names remain writing lines. The source OBEC emblem is copied without editing. No school or student records are changed by the layout.
