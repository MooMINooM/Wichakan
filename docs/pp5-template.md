# PP5 form reference

Reference: user-supplied `report5_V29.xls(2).xlsx`, print sheets `1.พิมพ์หน้าปก`, `2.พิมพ์เวลาเรียน`, `3.พิมพ์คะแนน`, `5.พิมพ์ ตชว`.

The printed form retains the source's emblem/title, inline course metadata, grade/count/percentage table, side-by-side assessment summaries, approval/signature boxes, four-level attendance headers and ruled 45-row rosters. Scores use portrait A4 and grouped indicators, exams, grades, eight traits and five reading criteria. Indicators retain the metadata and numbered writing area. Sarabun is embedded in the PDF.

User-directed adaptations take precedence over the original template: attendance has 25 Monday–Friday date columns in each five-week block, with multiple actual timetable periods in the same day cell. Nonteaching days are shaded. Dates beyond the first 20 weeks and rosters beyond 45 students continue rather than being cropped. Page numbers apply to the full book.

Scores/caps come from existing system records. Detailed pre/post-midterm indicator scores and retake scores are not collected by the current system; their source-template columns stay blank. The otherwise unused central-exam column displays the existing aggregated coursework score, with its heading changed to คะแนนเก็บรวม. The existing coursework and midterm values form the in-term total; finals and full-term total retain their own columns. No detail scores are invented or divided from aggregates. Annual primary grades remain pending until both terms are complete.

The original workbook's fixed staff names are not copied into live reports: current teacher/homeroom names come from system data, while unconfigured approval names remain writing lines. The source OBEC emblem is copied without editing. No school or student records are changed by the layout.
